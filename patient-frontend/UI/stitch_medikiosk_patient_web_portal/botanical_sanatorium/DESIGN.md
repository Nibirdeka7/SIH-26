## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a gentle, restorative interface tailored specifically for patient-facing pre-consultation kiosks and responsive web portals in Indian healthcare facilities. The aesthetic rejects the cold, anxiety-inducing clinical minimalism of traditional health software in favor of an organic, botanical sanctuary. It evokes the quiet dignity of Ayurvedic wellness spaces blended with contemporary civic infrastructure: grounded, human, reassuring, and profoundly trustworthy.

The style combines organic warmth with tactile physical metaphors. Light rice paper backdrops, soft clay partitions, and deep forest foliage hues set an immediate tone of de-escalation for patients who may be anxious, ill, or unfamiliar with digital kiosks. High-contrast typography paired with generous, deliberate touch targets ensures dignity and accessibility across multi-generational, multilingual populations. Visual motifs feature soft architectural arches, subtle paper-grain textures, and unhurried whitespace that respects the emotional state of the patient.

## Colors

The color palette grounds the digital experience in natural pigments:

- **Surface & Background (`#F9F8F4` - Warm Alabaster / Rice Paper):** The non-emissive, eye-resting canvas that prevents clinical glare under hospital fluorescent lighting.
- **Foreground / Text (`#2D3A31` - Deep Forest Slate):** High-contrast deep chlorophyll green used in place of harsh pure black for all structural text, icons, and primary boundaries. Achieves AAA contrast against light backgrounds while remaining soft and natural.
- **Botanical Mid-Tone (`#8C9A84` - Sage Green):** Used for non-urgent active states, structural chips, progress meters, and calm confirmations.
- **Secondary Neutral (`#DCCFC2` - Soft Clay / Mushroom):** Applied to subtle panel separators, disabled states, card outlines, and ambient depth layering.
- **Tactile Accent (`#C27B66` - Terracotta):** Direct call-to-action color for forward progression, primary submission, active voice capture, and critical interaction focal points.
- **Boundary Stone (`#E6E2DA`):** Low-contrast structural hairline divider for clean separation of information.
- **Clinical Attention (`#B33927` - Madder Red):** A restrained, earthy mineral red reserved exclusively for allergy warnings, triage escalation, and physiological critical alerts.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for floating question cards, touch pads, and input fields to elevate them above the alabaster floor.

## Typography

The typography pairs the editorial grace and warmth of **Playfair Display** with the utilitarian clarity of **Source Sans 3**. 

- **Headings (Playfair Display, 600 Weight):** Used for welcoming titles, stage announcements, section anchors, and key medical inquiries. This conveys human care, classic reassurance, and warmth, breaking away from intimidating robotic interfaces.
- **Body & Controls (Source Sans 3, 400 & 600 Weight):** Carries the interactive burden. Highly legible at rapid glance angles and distance on upright kiosks. Open counters, clear distinctions between `I`, `l`, and `1`, and generous proportions make it exceptionally accommodating for regional language transliteration and multilingual prompts.
- **Kiosk Readability Rule:** Running body text on pre-consultation prompt pages should never drop below `body-lg` (18px) to accommodate senior patients and standing interaction postures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an 8-point rhythmic grid with dedicated accommodations for fixed-height public touchscreens (landscape/portrait kiosks) and patient-owned mobile devices.

- **Kiosk Enclosure & Maximum Boundaries:** For wide screens (1024px and up), content is anchored within a centered max-width vessel of 960px to prevent excessive horizontal scanning. The primary interaction zone sits between 35% and 80% of vertical screen height, keeping buttons within ergonomic reach without stooping or high reaching.
- **Touch Ergonomics:** All primary interactive elements maintain a minimum target height of `3.5rem` (56px) with an absolute minimum tap threshold of `3rem` (48px). Between adjacent touch targets, provide at least `1rem` (16px) of negative space to prevent mis-touches by trembling or unsteady hands.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile (<= 640px)`: Single-column flow, 16px horizontal margins, sticky bottom action bar for navigation.
  - `Tablet / Portrait Kiosk (641px - 1024px)`: 8-column layout, 24px margins, stacked question cards with clear right-side control placement.
  - `Desktop / Landscape Kiosk (>= 1025px)`: 12-column layout, 32px margins, dual-pane architecture where visual context and summaries remain stationary on the left while questionnaires flow on the right.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic neon glows and harsh drop shadows. Visual depth is established through tonal paper layering and organic sunlight diffusion:

- **The Base Bed:** The deepest layer is the `#F9F8F4` Warm Alabaster floor, rendered with a subtle SVG microscopic fiber/grain pattern at 0.015 opacity to simulate unbleached deckle-edge paper.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Neutral Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces sitting directly upon the base layer, bounded by a crisp 1px hairline border in `#E6E2DA`. They employ an ambient diffusion shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Active Dialogs, Popovers & Key Selection Units):** Pure white container elevated with a deeper grounding cast: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.03)`.
- **Botanical Inset:** Input trays, inactive toggles, and selection wells use an inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px 0 rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.03)`) over a soft fill of `#F4F1EA` to evoke a physically debossed paper card.

## Shapes

The shape system merges tactile geometric softness with architectural tranquility. 

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standardized at an architectural radius of `24px` (`1.5rem`). This softens clinical anxiety and prevents harsh corners from feeling defensive.
- **Buttons, Badges, and Micro-Trays:** Built using the full pill silhouette (`rounded-full` / 9999px). Buttons invite physical pressing with complete perimeter curves.
- **The Sanatorium Arch:** Key section headers, intake category markers, and modal frames feature gentle arch motifs (e.g., top edges rounded at `32px` or full semi-circular headpieces) reflecting traditional Indian verandah arches and natural canopies.
- **Icons:** Rendered with lightweight 1.5px to 1.75px uniform strokes, matching the stroke density of `Source Sans 3` body copy. All terminal joins and corners in icon paths are rounded.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped, 56px height on kiosks (48px on mobile), filled with `#C27B66` (Terracotta), text in `#FFFFFF` with `label-lg` weight. Hover/Active transition deepens to `#A96552`. Accompanied by a gentle physical press translation (`translate-y-0.5`).
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped, 56px height, background `#8C9A84` at 12% opacity with a 1.5px `#8C9A84` border. Text in `#2D3A31`.
- **Voice Assist Button:** A dedicated 64px circular pill container floating or prominently anchored beside inputs. Uses `#8C9A84` with pulsating ambient concentric rings when active, paired with a tactile microphone icon and a bilingual "Tap to Speak / बोलें" label.

### Cards & Question Tiles
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` with a 24px corner radius and `#E6E2DA` border.
- **Symptom / Option Selector Card:** Generous padding (24px). Contains an optional organic botanical line illustration or icon, a large bold title (`Playfair Display`, 20px), and clear plain-language descriptions. When selected, the background turns to `#8C9A84` at 8% opacity, and the border transitions to a 2px stroke of `#2D3A31`.

### Chips & Tags
- Height: 36px to 44px for easy tap selection.
- Roundedness: Fully rounded pill (`rounded-full`).
- Unselected: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E6E2DA`, text `#2D3A31`.
- Selected: Surface `#2D3A31`, border 1px solid `#2D3A31`, text `#FFFFFF`. Includes a checkmark icon with 1.5px stroke.

### Input Fields & Triage Selectors
- Height: 56px. Background `#FFFFFF` with a 1px solid `#E6E2DA` perimeter border. Corner radius: 16px.
- Internal text uses `body-lg` (18px) for high visibility. Floating labels rest in `Source Sans 3` 14px uppercase with subtle letter-spacing.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#2D3A31` with a calm 3px halo in `#8C9A84` at 20% opacity. No high-vibration browser blue rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Sized at a touch-friendly 28px by 28px.
- Radio buttons: Fully round with a deep `#2D3A31` central botanical pip (14px) when active.
- Checkboxes: 8px border radius, transitioning to `#2D3A31` background with a crisp white tick mark on selection.

### Progress & Reassurance Bar
- A thin, organic tracker at the top of multi-step intake flows. Uses `#DCCFC2` as the track channel and `#8C9A84` as the organic progress fill, accompanied by calm human reassurance ("Step 2 of 4: Symptoms / लक्षण").
